I am Fernanda Chima, a development economist and the founder of InnovationsMX. My work brings together three things I care deeply about: international trade, technology and reducing inequality.

I lived in China from 2017 to 2023, where I studied International Economics at the University of International Business and Economics (UIBE) and earned a master's in Applied Economics at Xi'an Jiaotong University. I saw first-hand how manufacturing, e-commerce and logistics reshaped the way the world buys and sells.

As an Erasmus Mundus scholar, I went on to study the Economics of Globalization and European Integration at Ghent University (Belgium) and Quantitative Economics at the University of Tartu (Estonia). At the World Trade Organization I reviewed digital services and foreign investment policies and contributed to the 2025 World Trade Report on trade and artificial intelligence.

I bring more than eight years of consulting and program management experience across 30+ countries, with organizations including UNESCO, the United Nations Population Fund, the United Nations University, Huawei and the Hult Prize Foundation.

I founded InnovationsMX because Latin American small and medium enterprises deserve the same opportunities as large corporations: direct access to manufacturers, full visibility of their costs, and the strategy to compete through better services. That is our motto: **tomorrow's technology, in your hands today.**
